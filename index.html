        How to Play

        ↑↓
        Move the fish. Use the arrow keys (or W / S) to swim up and down.
        Avoid the rocks. Swim through the gaps to reach the finish line.
        Watch your lives. A rock costs 1 of 3 lives and sends you back to your last safe spot.
        Fish Count mini-game. Lose all 3 lives? Count the fish correctly to restart the level with full lives.
